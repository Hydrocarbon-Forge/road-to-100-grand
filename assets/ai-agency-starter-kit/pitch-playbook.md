# Pitch Playbook — Cold Outreach That Gets Replies

Principles: short, specific, human. Prove you looked at their business in the
first two lines. One concrete idea, one low-pressure ask. Never send identical
blasts. Personalize every send.

Volume target: 5–10 pitches/day. Track everything. Follow up once after 4–5
business days, then move on.

Replace all `[brackets]` before sending.

---

## Email Template 1 — The Site Teardown (best for businesses with a dated site)

Subject: quick idea for `[BusinessName]`'s site

> Hi `[Name]`,
>
> I found `[BusinessName]` through `[where — Google Maps / referral / group]`
> and noticed `[one specific observation: "your services page doesn't list
> prices or a booking link, so mobile visitors have to call"]`.
>
> I build fast, simple websites for `[niche, e.g., local trades]`. One idea:
> `[concrete fix, e.g., "a one-page quote form with photos of past jobs
> typically lifts quote requests — I could mock that up for you"]`.
>
> Open to a $5 idea call this week (60 min, you keep the plan either way)?
> I have `[Tue/Thu]` open.
>
> — `[Your Name]`, `[Studio]`
> `[portfolio link or GitHub]`

Why it works: specific observation + one idea + cheap, defined next step.

## Objection handling (6)

**1. "Too expensive."**
> Totally fair. The quote is fixed — `[scope]` for `$[total]`, nothing added
> later. If budget's the blocker, we can cut `[one named feature]` and re-quote
> at `$[lower]`. Want the trimmed version, or should we park this?

**2. "Can't we just hire a dev / have our nephew do it?"**
> You could — and for ongoing product work you should. What I sell is a
> finished, launched marketing site in `[timeline]` with a fixed price, handoff
> docs, and a bug-fix window. No recruiting, no managing, no hourly meter.
> If you have someone in-house, my $5 scope plan still helps them — yours to keep.

**3. "Why not use [Wix/Squarespace/template site]?"**
> If a template covers everything you need, use it — seriously. Custom pays
> off when one conversion goal matters (`[their goal]`) and the template
> fights you on speed, structure, or checkout. Happy to tell you honestly on
> the call if you're a template case — the $5 plan works either way.

**4. "We need it faster."**
> Rush is possible: `[+X%]` for delivery by `[date]`, same scope, same
> revision limit. The constraint is always content — if you can get me
> text/photos/logo by `[date]`, I'll hit `[date]`. Deal?

**5. "Send me a proposal first / just give me a price."**
> I quote fixed prices, but only from a real scope — guessing from the outside
> is how projects go wrong. The $5 call (60 min) produces the itemized quote;
> if you don't build with me, the plan still helps whoever you hire. Fair?

**6. "We're not ready / maybe later."**
> No problem — bad timing kills more projects than bad builders. I'll close
> this out; if `[their goal]` becomes urgent, reply anytime and we pick up
> from the scope we discussed. One email from me, then silence unless you ping.
