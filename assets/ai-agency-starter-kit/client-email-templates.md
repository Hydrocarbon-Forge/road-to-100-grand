# Client Email Templates — 10 Copy-Paste Emails

How to use: copy, replace `[brackets]`, keep the tone. Each is intentionally
short — clients skim.

---

## 1. Lead reply (first response to an interested prospect)

Subject: Re: `[original subject]`

> Hi `[Name]`,
>
> Thanks for replying. Quick next step: I do a $5, 60-minute idea call where
> we map your pages, features, and content, and you get a fixed quote with
> an itemized breakdown — yours to keep either way.
>
> I have `[two time options]` open this week. Either work?
>
> — `[Your Name]`, `[Studio]`

## 2. Welcome (after they say yes to a call / project)

Subject: Next steps for `[BusinessName]`'s site

> Hi `[Name]`,
>
> Excited to build this with you. Here's what happens next:
>
> 1. Fill this short intake (5 min): `[intake link or pasted questions]`
> 2. Our scoping call: `[date/time + link]`
> 3. After the call I'll send your fixed quote + timeline.
>
> To get the most from the call, have 2–3 sites you like ready (even rough
> examples help).
>
> — `[Your Name]`

## 3. Scoping (agenda + reminder before the call)

Subject: Agenda for our call `[date]`

> Hi `[Name]`,
>
> Tomorrow's call (`[time + link]`, 60 min) covers:
>
> - Your #1 goal for the site
> - Pages + features list
> - Who provides text/photos, and by when
> - Live quote + timeline
>
> Please send any logo/photos/text you already have before the call if you
> can — even drafts are fine.
>
> — `[Your Name]`

## 4. Proposal (fixed quote after scoping call)

Subject: Fixed quote: `[BusinessName]` — `[scope summary]` — `$[total]`

> Hi `[Name]`,
>
> Great call today. Here's your fixed quote (valid 14 days):
>
> **Scope:** `[e.g., 4 pages: Home, Services, About, Contact + quote form + analytics]`
> **Price breakdown:**
> `[paste calculator output — e.g., Base $150 + 4 pages × $80 = $320 + quote form $60 + ... = $X]`
> **Total: $[total]** (50% deposit to start, 50% before launch)
> **Timeline:** `[e.g., 2–3 weeks from deposit + content received]`
> **Includes:** 2 revision rounds, mobile-responsive build, basic SEO + analytics, launch + handoff doc, 14-day bug-fix window.
>
> To proceed: reply "approved" and I'll send the agreement + deposit invoice.
> Work starts when both are complete.
>
> — `[Your Name]`

## 5. Follow-up (4–5 days after proposal, send once)

Subject: Checking in — `[BusinessName]` proposal

> Hi `[Name]`,
>
> Checking in on the proposal (`[scope]` at `$[total]`). Still a fit, or
> should I close this out?
>
> Either way is fine — just don't want to leave you hanging.
>
> — `[Your Name]`

## 6. Payment (deposit invoice + contract)

Subject: Agreement + deposit for `[BusinessName]` — let's start

> Hi `[Name]`,
>
> Attached: (1) service agreement, (2) deposit invoice for `$[deposit]`
> (50% of `$[total]`).
>
> Steps: sign the agreement → pay the deposit → I'll confirm your build
> slot and content deadline (`[date]`).
>
> Content I need from you by `[date]`: `[text / photos / logo list]`.
> No content, no build start — delays shift launch day-for-day.
>
> — `[Your Name]`

## 7. Delivery (site launched, balance due before handoff)

Subject: `[BusinessName]` is live 🎉 + final steps

> Hi `[Name]`,
>
> `[BusinessName]` is live at `[URL]` — tested on mobile, forms working,
> analytics on.
>
> Attached: (1) balance invoice `$[balance]`, (2) your handoff doc (logins,
> how to edit text/images, what to do if something breaks).
>
> Quick favor after you've clicked around: reply with one line I can quote
> about working together?
>
> — `[Your Name]`

## 8. Upsell (7–14 days after launch: care plan)

Subject: Want me to keep an eye on `[BusinessName]`?

> Hi `[Name]`,
>
> Your 14-day bug-fix window closes `[date]` — anything off versus our scope,
> tell me before then and it's free.
>
> After that I offer a care plan at `$[X]`/mo: updates, backups checks,
> small content tweaks, and monitoring. No lock-in, cancel anytime.
>
> Want it, or happy to self-manage with the handoff doc?
>
> — `[Your Name]`

## 9. Referral (ask when they're happy — after testimonial or kind words)

Subject: Who else needs this?

> Hi `[Name]`,
>
> Glad `[BusinessName]` is working out. Quick ask: do you know one
> `[niche, e.g., trade business / creator]` still stuck with `[their old
> problem, e.g., "a Facebook-only presence"]`?
>
> An intro (CC me) gets them a scoped $5 call, and gets you `[thank-you —
> e.g., one free care-plan month / $25 credit]` if they build with me.
>
> — `[Your Name]`

## 10. Winback (past client or stalled lead, 60–90 days later, send once)

Subject: Still thinking about `[scope / a new site]`?

> Hi `[Name]`,
>
> It's been a while — hope `[Business]` is going well. I'm opening `[e.g.,
> two]` build slots for `[month]` and thought of you.
>
> If `[original goal]` is still on the list, the $5 scoping call + fixed
> quote still stand — plan's yours either way. If timing's still off, just
> say so and I'll close this out.
>
> — `[Your Name]`
