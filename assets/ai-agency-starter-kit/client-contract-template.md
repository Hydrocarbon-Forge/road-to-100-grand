**Between:** `[Your Name / Studio]` ("Builder") and `[Client Name / Business]` ("Client")
**Date:** `[date]` · **Project:** `[one-line scope, e.g., "5-page site for X Plumbing"]`

## 1. Scope (exactly what IS included)

- Pages: `[list each page]`
- Features: `[list each feature, e.g., quote form, booking embed]`
- Also included: mobile-responsive build, basic SEO + analytics setup, launch + handoff doc, `[INCLUDED_REVISIONS, default 2]` revision rounds, 14-day bug-fix window (below).

Out of scope (billed separately if requested): `[e.g., logo design, copywriting beyond 1 polish pass, e-commerce, integrations not listed]`.

## 2. Price & payment

- **Total fixed price:** `$[total]` (quote valid 14 days from `[date]`).
- **50% deposit (`$[deposit]`)** due with the signed agreement to schedule the build.
- **50% balance (`$[balance]`)** due before launch/transfer. No launch before final payment clears.
- $5 idea-call fee credited toward the total if the client proceeds within `[14]` days.

## 3. Content & timeline

- Client delivers text, photos, and logo by `[content deadline]`. No content, no build start; delays push the timeline day-for-day.
- Estimated timeline: `[e.g., 2–3 weeks]` from deposit + content received. Rush (`[+X%]`) delivery date if agreed: `[date]`.

## 4. Revisions

- `[2]` revision rounds included. One round = one consolidated feedback list.
- Extra rounds at `$[rate]` each, quoted before work starts. Redesigns (new direction after approval) are re-quoted as new scope.

## 5. Bug-fix window & support

- 14 days after launch: bugs **versus the agreed scope** fixed free.
- Changes (new content, features, redesign) are quoted separately.
- Optional care plan: `$[X]`/mo for updates + monitoring (separate agreement).

## 6. Ownership & cancellation

- Client owns the finished site and content on final payment; Builder may list the work in a portfolio unless the client opts out in writing.
- Either side may cancel in writing. Work completed + deposit are non-refundable; if the client cancels mid-build, the deposit covers scoping + work done, balance for undelivered work is waived.
